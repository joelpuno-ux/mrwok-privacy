## 1. Who we are

Mr. Wok is a Filipino-Chinese food business with branches in **Cabiao, Nueva
Ecija** and **Gapan City, Nueva Ecija**, Philippines. We operate the Mr. Wok
Customer app, which lets customers order our food for **delivery** or **pick-up**
and pay by **Cash on Delivery**, **Cash on Pick up**, or **GCash**.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information**
- Name
- Mobile number
- Email address
- Password — stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider
  (Supabase). We never see or store your password in readable form.
- Account identifier (a random user ID created when you sign up)

**Delivery information**
- House number, street, barangay and landmark / delivery notes
- The GPS location you pin on the map, and your device’s location when you tap
  “Use my current location”

**Order information**
- The items you order, quantities and prices
- Food subtotal, delivery fee and grand total
- Order status and timestamps (placed, accepted, preparing, out for delivery,
  delivered)
- The branch you ordered from and the service mode (delivery or pick-up)

**Payment information**
- Whether an order has been paid, the payment method used (Cash or GCash), and
  the payment gateway’s reference number
- **We do not collect or store your GCash credentials, card numbers, or bank
  account details.** Online payments are completed on the payment gateway’s own
  secure page (PayMongo / Xendit), and we only receive a confirmation and a
  reference number.

**Feedback**
- The star ratings and comments you submit through the Feedback & Rating tab

**We do not collect:** advertising identifiers, device identifiers, contacts,
photos, files, calendar data, SMS, call logs, browsing history, health data, or
precise background location.

## 3. How we use your information

- To prepare, deliver and confirm your order
- To calculate the delivery fee from the distance between you and the branch
- To let our branch staff and your assigned rider contact you about your order
- To create and manage your account and show your order history
- To process and verify online (GCash) payments
- To respond to your feedback and improve our food and service
- To keep records required for accounting and legal purposes

We do **not** use your information for advertising, and we do **not** sell it.


## 4. Who we share it with

**Our own staff and riders.** The branch staff preparing your order and the
assigned delivery rider receive only what they need to complete the delivery:
your name, contact number, delivery address, landmark, notes and map pin.

**Service providers who process data on our behalf** (they may not use it for
their own purposes):
- **Supabase** — secure cloud database and authentication (account, order and
  feedback data)
- **PayMongo / Xendit** — GCash payment processing
- **OpenStreetMap** and, if enabled, **Google Maps** — map tiles and distance
  calculation for the delivery fee
- **Twilio** (if SMS alerts are enabled) — order status text messages

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** share it with
advertisers, data brokers or analytics companies.

## 5. Location data

The app asks for location permission **only while you are using it** (foreground
only — never in the background). We use it to:
- pin your delivery address on the map, and
- compute the distance to the branch so we can calculate the delivery fee.

Your delivery location is shared with our branch staff and your assigned rider so
they can find you. You can turn the permission off at any time in **Android
Settings → Apps → Mr. Wok Customer → Permissions → Location**; the menu and your
account still work, but **delivery orders cannot be completed without it**
(pick-up orders and account access are unaffected).

## 6. Payments

Cash payments are collected by our rider or at the branch. Online payments are
handled entirely by our payment gateway on their own secure, PCI-DSS compliant
page; the app never sees your GCash credentials. If an online-paid order cannot
be fulfilled, we refund the full amount to the same GCash account within
**3–7 working days**.

## 7. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted **HTTPS** connections and stored on
access-controlled servers. Access is limited to staff who need it to serve you.
We do not permit plain HTTP (cleartext) traffic in the app.

## 8. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and order data for as long as your account is active, and
afterwards only for the period required for accounting, tax and legal purposes.
When it is no longer needed, we delete it or turn it into anonymous statistics.

## 9. Your rights

You can:
- **See and correct** your details inside the app (Account tab)
- **Request deletion** of your account and personal data at any time — see the
  [Account Deletion page](account-deletion.md), or use the **Feedback & Rating**
  tab in the app, or contact us at <<SUPPORT EMAIL>>

We action verified deletion requests within **30 days**.

## 10. Children

The app is intended for users **18 years of age and older**. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
